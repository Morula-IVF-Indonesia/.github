# Morula IVF Indonesia

Founded in 1997, Morula IVF Indonesia has grown into one of the first and
leading fertility centers in Indonesia.

This is where our technology team builds the digital services around our
clinics: tools that help people understand their fertility and reach our
team, and the internal systems that support our staff across branches.

### Our principles

- **Patients' privacy first.** Health data is collected only with consent,
  kept to what is needed, and never written to logs.
- **Built to be maintained.** Reviewed changes, automated checks and
  documented deployments for every service.
